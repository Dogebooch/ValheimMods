# Backpack Upgrade Guide

## Overview
The Backpacks mod provides a single, upgradable backpack system that scales with your progression through Valheim. Each upgrade level increases storage capacity and provides consistent carry weight bonuses. The system has been consolidated to eliminate conflicts and provide meaningful progression.

> **Note**: While backpack storage capacity increases with each upgrade level, the carry weight bonus remains consistent at +50 weight and 20% weight reduction. This is due to technical limitations of the Backpacks mod, which only supports a single status effect.

> **Important**: The quiver system has been completely disabled to prevent conflicts with the consolidated backpack system. All quiver recipes are disabled and upgrades are blocked.

## Backpack Progression

### Level 1: Basic Backpack
- **Slots**: 2×3 (6 total)
- **Requirements**: Workbench Level 3
- **Materials**: BronzeNails:5, DeerHide:10, LeatherScraps:10
- **Tier**: Bronze Age
- **Carry Bonus**: +50 weight, 20% weight reduction

### Level 2: Iron Backpack  
- **Slots**: 3×4 (12 total)
- **Requirements**: Workbench Level 3
- **Materials**: Iron:8, ElderBark:15, **TrophyEikthyr:1**
- **Tier**: Iron Age
- **Carry Bonus**: +50 weight, 20% weight reduction
- **Note**: Requires defeating Eikthyr

### Level 3: Silver Backpack
- **Slots**: 4×4 (16 total)  
- **Requirements**: Workbench Level 3
- **Materials**: Silver:8, WolfPelt:5, **TrophyBonemass:1**
- **Tier**: Silver Age
- **Carry Bonus**: +50 weight, 20% weight reduction
- **Note**: Requires defeating Bonemass

### Level 4: BlackMetal Backpack
- **Slots**: 4×6 (24 total)
- **Requirements**: Workbench Level 3
- **Materials**: BlackMetal:8, LinenThread:15, **TrophyModer:1**
- **Tier**: BlackMetal Age
- **Carry Bonus**: +50 weight, 20% weight reduction
- **Note**: Requires defeating Moder

### Level 5: Endgame Backpack
- **Slots**: 5×7 (35 total)
- **Requirements**: Workbench Level 3
- **Materials**: JuteBlue:8, ScaleHide:8, **TrophyYagluth:1**
- **Tier**: Endgame
- **Carry Bonus**: +50 weight, 20% weight reduction
- **Note**: Requires defeating Yagluth

## Benefits

### Storage Capacity
- **Level 1**: 6 slots (starter)
- **Level 2**: 12 slots (early game, +100%)
- **Level 3**: 16 slots (mid game, +33%)
- **Level 4**: 24 slots (late game, +50%)
- **Level 5**: 35 slots (endgame, +46%)

### Status Effects
- **BackpackCarryBonus**: Single status effect provides consistent benefits:
  - **All levels**: +50 carry weight, 20% weight reduction for items in backpack
- **Auto-fill**: Items automatically go to backpack when picked up
- **Auto-open**: Backpack opens with inventory
- **Visual**: Backpack model hidden (no clipping)

## Other Carry Weight Items

### EpicLoot Belts (Shoulder & Utility Slots)
These items provide additional carry weight bonuses and can be found as magic items:

- **Leather Belt**: +25 carry weight (Tier 1, common)
- **Silver Belt**: +200 carry weight (Tier 4, rare)
- **BlackMetal Belt**: +250 carry weight (Tier 5, very rare)
- **Dvergr Belt**: +300 carry weight (Tier 6, legendary)

**Note**: EpicLoot belts can roll on both Shoulder and Utility slots, allowing for flexible equipment combinations with backpacks.

### Carry Capacity Adjuncts
These items provide additional carry weight bonuses and are properly tier-gated for game progression:

- **BeltStrength**: +150 carry weight (Blacksmithing Level 25 - Bronze Age)
- **Philosopher Stones (Red/Blue/Green/Purple)**: +75 carry weight each (Blacksmithing Level 35 - Silver Age)
  - **Crafting**: Requires Potion Cauldron Level 3 + Elder defeated
  - **Materials**: Flask variants + Iron (10) + Silver (5)
- **FishExtract**: +250 carry weight (Fishing Level 50 - Mid-game)
- **Philosopher Stone Black**: +250 carry weight (Blacksmithing Level 80 - Endgame)
  - **Crafting**: Requires Potion Cauldron Level 5 + Seeker Queen defeated
  - **Materials**: Flask of the Gods (10) + BlackMetal (20) + YmirRemains (10)
- **GP_Fader**: +300 carry weight (Blacksmithing Level 90 - Ultimate endgame)

### Food Effects
- **Lox Jerky**: +10 carry weight (temporary bonus from food)

### How to Obtain

#### EpicLoot Belts
- **Drops**: Found as magic items from creatures and chests
- **Gambling**: Available through EpicLoot's gambling system
- **Rarity**: Higher-tier belts are significantly rarer and more expensive
- **Tier Gating**: Belts are gated by world level and boss progression

#### Carry Capacity Adjuncts
- **Philosopher Stones**: Crafted at Potion Cauldron (custom crafting station)
  - **Lower-tier Stones**: Level 3 cauldron + Elder defeated + Flask variants + Iron + Silver
  - **Black Stone**: Level 5 cauldron + Seeker Queen defeated + Flask of the Gods + BlackMetal + YmirRemains
- **BeltStrength**: Found as EpicLoot items or drops (no standalone recipe)
- **FishExtract**: Found as EpicLoot items or drops (no standalone recipe)
- **GP_Fader**: Found as EpicLoot items or drops (no standalone recipe)

#### Food Effects
- **Lox Jerky**: Crafted at cooking station using Lox Meat and Honey
- **Duration**: Temporary effect while food buff is active

## Upgrade Strategy

### Early Game (Bronze → Iron)
- Focus on defeating Eikthyr first
- Eikthyr trophy is required for Level 2 backpack
- Iron backpack provides significant storage increase

### Mid Game (Iron → Silver)
- Defeat Bonemass for Level 3 upgrade
- Silver backpack offers 16 slots for extended exploration
- Carry weight bonus increases to +55

### Late Game (Silver → BlackMetal)
- Moder fight required for Level 4 backpack
- 24 slots provide substantial storage for endgame content
- +70 carry weight bonus significantly improves mobility

### Endgame (BlackMetal → Ultimate)
- Yagluth fight required for final upgrade
- 35 slots provide maximum storage capacity
- +85 carry weight and 30% weight reduction for optimal performance

## Balance Notes

- **No Stacking Conflicts**: Backpack and belt effects are designed to work together without overpowering combinations
- **Progressive Difficulty**: Each upgrade requires defeating progressively harder bosses
- **Material Costs**: Upgrade materials scale with tier and include boss trophies for meaningful progression
- **EpicLoot Integration**: Belts are properly tier-gated and rarity-balanced for fair progression
